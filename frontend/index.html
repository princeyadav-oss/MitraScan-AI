ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Technology with a Practical Purpose

MitraScan AI is a student-developed prototype designed to assist with preliminary screening of packaged commodity labels.

The project combines a web interface, OCR, information extraction, AI-assisted analysis and a configurable rule engine.
